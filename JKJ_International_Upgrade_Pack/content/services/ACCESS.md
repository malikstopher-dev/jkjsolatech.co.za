## Intro
JKJ designs access-control systems for single doors, gates and multi-entry properties, with options for biometrics, cards, PINs, intercoms and integration with CCTV and gate automation.

## What we deliver
- Biometric fingerprint readers
- Face-recognition systems where appropriate
- Proximity cards and key fobs
- PIN and keypad entry
- Audio and video intercoms
- IP intercoms with mobile integration
- Multi-door access-control systems
- Access logs and reporting
- Gate and barrier integration
- CCTV and alarm integration
- Visitor-management solutions
- System upgrades and expansion

## Typical applications

- Estates
- Residential properties
- Retail
- Commercial and multi-tenant buildings
