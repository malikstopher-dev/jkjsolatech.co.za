Our Journey

Built by Friends. Driven by a Greater Vision.

JKJ Solartech and Security (Pty) Ltd was founded by three friends with a shared passion for technology, security and sustainable energy. Our roots are in South Africa, and one of our partners is on the ground in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

We serve residential, commercial, industrial and institutional clients with a multi-disciplinary in-house team covering solar, electrical, CCTV, access control, gates, smart systems, IT and web design.
