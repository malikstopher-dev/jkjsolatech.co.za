About JKJ Solartech and Security

A registered Johannesburg company delivering integrated energy, security and technology solutions across Gauteng and the DRC market.

Our Story

Who We Are

JKJ Solartech and Security (Pty) Ltd was founded on a straightforward premise: property owners in Johannesburg deserve a single, reliable provider that handles energy, security, connectivity and trade — done properly, without subcontracts or excuses.

From solar backup systems and electrical infrastructure to CCTV, automated gates, access control, smart home systems, IT networking, web design and electronics import/export — we cover everything under one roof. One company. No middlemen. Every installation completed by our own team to South African National Standards.

We serve residential homes, commercial properties, places of worship and industrial sites across Johannesburg and greater Gauteng, and we are expanding into the Democratic Republic of Congo through structured market development partnerships.

We power properties. We protect people. We build confidence.

How We Work

Our Approach

Every project starts with a site assessment and a clear, itemised quote. No vague estimates, no hidden line items. We survey the property, identify exactly what is needed, recommend the right equipment and give you a fixed price before any work begins.

Our installations follow applicable South African National Standards. Electrical work is completed to SANS 10142. Solar systems comply with SANS 10142-3. Security installations are designed to industry benchmarks for reliability and coverage.

We keep the work clean. Cables are trunked and concealed. Panels are mounted with proper spacing. Terminals are labelled. Every job leaves the site looking professional — because that is what we would accept in our own homes.

After installation, you receive full documentation: compliance certificates, warranty information, system manuals and direct contact details for ongoing support. We do not disappear after the final invoice.
