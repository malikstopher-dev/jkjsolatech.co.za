What We Offer

Our IT & Networking Services

JKJ Solartech and Security delivers robust, scalable IT networking solutions to businesses across Johannesburg. From small office LAN setups to enterprise-grade fibre optic installations, our network engineers design and deploy infrastructure that keeps your business connected and performing at its best.

In today's digitally-driven world, reliable network infrastructure is non-negotiable. We handle every aspect of your connectivity needs — from initial consultation and cable pathway design through to installation, testing and ongoing maintenance.

Services Include:

  • Structured cabling installation (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, Cat7)
  • Single-mode and multi-mode fibre optic installation
  • LAN and WAN network design and setup
  • Enterprise Wi-Fi deployment and heat mapping
  • Server room design, build and cable management
  • Patch panel installation and labelling
  • Data centre cabling and infrastructure
  • Network troubleshooting and diagnostics
  • Network rack installation and organisation
  • Fibre splicing, termination and testing
  • Cable auditing and infrastructure documentation
