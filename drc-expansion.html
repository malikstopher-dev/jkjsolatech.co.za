DRC Market Development

JKJ Solartech is exploring opportunities in the Democratic Republic of Congo

Our Expansion Vision

Expanding into the DRC Market

JKJ Solartech and Security is actively exploring market development opportunities in the Democratic Republic of Congo. As one of Africa's largest and most resource-rich nations, the DRC represents a significant growth opportunity for our integrated electrical, security and technology services.

With a population exceeding 100 million people and rapid urbanisation in cities like Kinshasa, Lubumbashi and Mbuji-Mayi, the DRC has a growing demand for reliable electrical infrastructure, modern security solutions and IT connectivity. JKJ Solartech is positioning itself to meet this demand by establishing strategic partnerships and building relationships with local stakeholders.

The Kinshasa Opportunity

Kinshasa, the capital city, is one of the largest metropolitan areas in Africa with over 17 million residents. The city is experiencing significant growth in construction, commercial development and infrastructure investment. This creates substantial demand for the services JKJ Solartech specialises in:

  • Electrical installations for new commercial and residential developments
  • Solar energy systems to address power reliability challenges
  • CCTV and security systems for businesses, hotels and residential estates
  • Automated gate systems for the growing number of secure complexes
  • IT networking and fibre infrastructure for businesses and telecoms
  • Smart building and home automation solutions

Planned Services for the DRC

Subject to our market development timeline and regulatory approvals, JKJ Solartech plans to offer the following services in the DRC market:

  • Commercial and residential electrical installations
  • Solar power and battery backup solutions
  • CCTV, access control and security system installations
  • Automated gate and perimeter security solutions
  • Structured cabling and network infrastructure
  • Electronics wholesale distribution
  • Technical consulting and project management
  • Ongoing maintenance and support services

Note: JKJ Solartech does not currently have an office or operational presence in the DRC. This page describes our planned expansion and market development activities. All future operations will be subject to applicable regulatory requirements in both South Africa and the DRC.
