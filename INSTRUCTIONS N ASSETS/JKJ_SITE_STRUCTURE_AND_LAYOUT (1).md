## Homepage layout

### 1. Utility bar
Very slim. Johannesburg, phone, email, optional “South Africa | DRC opportunities”.

### 2. Premium sticky header
Transparent over hero, becomes solid charcoal on scroll.
Logo left. Navigation centered/right. Gold quote CTA.

### 3. Hero
Full-bleed image, approximately 80–90vh on desktop and 72–82vh on mobile.

Headline:
**Powering Your World. Protecting Your Future.**

Supporting line:
**Integrated energy, security, connectivity and digital solutions for homes, businesses and projects across South Africa, with market development underway in the DRC.**

Buttons:
- Request a Quote
- Explore Services

Do not place fake metrics in the hero.

### 4. Credibility strip
Use factual items only:
- Registered South African company
- Johannesburg-based
- Multi-service capability
- Nationwide project support subject to scope

### 5. Services editorial grid
Eight or nine services grouped into four families:
- Energy
- Security & Access
- Connectivity & Digital
- Trade & Expansion

Use image-led editorial tiles with varied sizes instead of nine identical cards.

### 6. Integrated-solutions section
Explain that JKJ can combine:
- power + CCTV
- gates + access control
- security + networking
- solar + digital monitoring
- procurement + installation

This section is important because it makes the company feel like an integrated solutions partner, not a list of trades.

### 7. DRC / Africa expansion feature
Large visual split section with South Africa and DRC framing.

Use wording such as:
> “From South Africa to the DRC: developing partnerships, sourcing opportunities and project relationships.”

Do not say there is a DRC office unless one actually exists.

### 8. Sectors served
Residential, commercial, retail, offices, estates/complexes, churches/places of worship, hospitality, light industrial, project-based work.

### 9. Projects / capability proof
Only use real projects. If real case studies are not ready, show a restrained “Project gallery coming soon / Request references” module rather than invented work.

### 10. Three-friends story
Image or abstract founder visual.

Headline:
**Built by Three Friends. Driven by One Bigger Vision.**

Explain the founding story and complementary skills. Mention that one partner is developing business opportunities and partnerships in the DRC.

### 11. Process
Discover → Assess → Design → Quote → Deliver → Support

### 12. Final CTA
Short and confident:
**Have a site, security, energy or technology project? Let’s scope it properly.**

### 13. Footer
Logo, services, company links, South Africa contact, DRC partnership enquiries, legal/privacy.

# Service Page Blueprint

Each service page should use the same architecture but different content.

1. Cinematic service hero
2. Short capability statement
3. “What we deliver” list
4. Typical applications / client types
5. Why JKJ / integration advantages
6. Related services
7. Small FAQ
8. Quote CTA / WhatsApp CTA

Avoid long walls of text and avoid repeating the same six-card grid on every service page.

# Visual Language

## Palette
- Near black: `#05070A`
- Charcoal: `#0B0F14`
- Soft charcoal: `#131820`
- JKJ gold: `#F5C300`
- Warm gold: `#D9A800`
- Off-white: `#F5F5F2`
- Muted grey: `#9CA3AF`

## Typography
Recommended:
- Display/headlines: **Sora**, **Space Grotesk** or **Manrope**
- Body/UI: **Inter** or **DM Sans**

Do not use condensed display fonts for every paragraph. Large headlines, quiet body copy.

## Spacing
- max content width: 1280–1360px
- generous section padding: 96–140px desktop
- 64–88px tablet
- 48–72px mobile
- card radius: 16–24px maximum
- thin 1px borders with low opacity

## Motion
- subtle image zoom on hover
- 12–24px reveal movement
- 300–600ms timing
- no excessive parallax
- no flash transition on every link
- respect reduced motion

## Mobile
Design mobile intentionally; do not simply stack the desktop.
- large tap targets
- sticky quote/WhatsApp option can be considered
- service tiles become two-column where space permits, one column for text-heavy sections
- keep hero copy under ~8 lines
