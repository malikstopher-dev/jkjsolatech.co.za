## Our story

JKJ was created by three friends with complementary interests and skills. The business started from a practical idea: clients should be able to work with one accountable team across several systems that affect how a property or business operates.

That means looking beyond a single installation. Power affects security. Security depends on reliable connectivity. Businesses also need digital visibility, technology equipment and ongoing support. JKJ is being built around that connected view.

## South Africa + DRC

JKJ is based in South Africa and is developing market relationships in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. One founding partner is travelling into the DRC to explore business opportunities, identify project needs and establish relationships with potential clients and partners.

The company should present this accurately as **business development and expansion activity**, not as an established DRC branch until that becomes fact.
