Electronics Import & Export

Strategic electronics sourcing, distribution and trade solutions across South Africa and Africa

What We Offer

Our Trade Services

JKJ Solartech and Security provides comprehensive electronics import and export services, connecting South African businesses with global suppliers and African markets. Leveraging our established supply chain networks and deep understanding of cross-border trade regulations, we help businesses source quality electronics at competitive prices and distribute them efficiently across the continent.

From sourcing and procurement through to logistics, customs clearance and final delivery, we manage the entire trade cycle. Our experience in both the South African and broader African markets means we understand the unique challenges of electronics trade on the continent and can navigate complex regulatory environments on your behalf.

Services Include:

  • Global electronics sourcing and procurement
  • Import logistics and freight management
  • Wholesale electronics distribution in South Africa
  • Supplier identification, vetting and management
  • Customs clearance and compliance (SARS)
  • Quality assurance and pre-shipment inspection
  • Bulk procurement for commercial and industrial clients
  • Export of electronics to African markets (DRC, Zambia, Mozambique)
  • Warehousing and inventory management
  • Trade documentation and regulatory compliance
  • Market intelligence and sourcing advisory
